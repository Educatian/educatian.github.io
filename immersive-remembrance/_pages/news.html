Sep 26, 2026

3D archive gallery goes public

Seven interactive records are now online in a mobile-friendly gallery: the Brown Bess musket, a machine-gun T&E mechanism, the Gold Star memorial, the Chosin Reservoir diorama, the "Maytear" field washer, a Pershing II launcher and a map of the museum site. Each object's 3D files can be downloaded.

Sep 25, 2026

Chosin Reservoir diorama reconstructed

The museum's Chosin diorama now has a 3D counterpart. It is matched to the gallery photograph, set on real terrain, and has a walk-in view and a historical-kit mode with rigged Marines and vehicles checked against 1950 equipment.

Sep 25, 2026

Gold Star memorial shared with family permission

With the permission of AVMA and the families, the memorial display for Maj. William E. Winter and LCpl Adam E. Loggins became a virtual vitrine. Home addresses and tag inscriptions are blurred.

Sep 23, 2026

First visit to the museum

The team visited the Alabama Veterans Museum & Archives in Athens to meet museum leadership, discuss priorities, collections and roles, and photograph gallery objects. Those photographs became the first 3D records.

Sep 2, 2026

IMLS award begins

The University of Alabama received notice of the IMLS National Leadership Grant for Museums award (MG-260576-OMS-26) that funds Immersive Remembrance through 2029.
