What you can use

Available now

3D archive models

A .glb model, metadata, research notes and build scripts for each object. Also available one object at a time from the 3D Archive page.

  • Brown Bess musket (2.1 MB)
  • Machine-gun T&E (0.5 MB)
  • Gold Star memorial (3.4 MB)
  • Chosin Reservoir diorama (8.1 MB)
  • "Maytear" field washer
  • Pershing II launcher
Planned · Year 3

Living Archive VR Framework

The Unity/C# engine, the RAG retrieval pipeline in Python, and configuration templates, released on GitHub under the MIT License.

Planned · Year 3

"Zero-to-Hero" adoption manual

A step-by-step guide for museum staff to deploy the framework with off-the-shelf headsets, published on GitHub and OSF under CC BY 4.0.

Planned · Year 3

Dataset & codebook

The de-identified visitor study dataset and analysis codebook, deposited on OSF with a persistent DOI.

Planned · Year 1

Open architecture report

A technical report describing the framework design and beta test results.

Planned · Years 2–3

Papers & workshops

Peer-reviewed publications, plus hands-on framework workshops at the AAM Annual Meeting and the ASTC conference.

How to cite this project

Moon, J., Hu, F., & Kim, J. (2026–2029). Immersive Remembrance: AI-driven archival retrieval for VR learning [IMLS National Leadership Grant for Museums, MG-260576-OMS-26]. The University of Alabama. https://educatian.github.io/immersive-remembrance/
