# educatian.github.io

Landing site for **Educatian**: open guides for educational research methods (learning analytics, knowledge tracing, ontologies and qualitative coding, AI-assisted research workflows) plus hosted project pages and presentations.

**Status:** active

**Live:** https://educatian.github.io/

## Published project pages

| Project | Public page | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher Response Simulation | https://educatian.github.io/teacher-response-simulation/ | Korean elementary classroom Unity prototype for generative-AI-supported teacher response training, including the research purpose, interaction evidence, research tracks, roadmap, team, and ethical boundaries. |

The Teacher Response Simulation page is based on the project's public-safe 2026–2029 research plan and current prototype evidence. Planned evaluation activities and outputs are labeled as plans rather than completed results.
